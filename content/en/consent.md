This consent applies to messages sent through the contact form at karmanto.com. By ticking the consent box and
sending the form, I freely, for a specific purpose and in an informed manner give my consent to **Aleksandr Serenko**
(the operator, support@karmanto.com) to process my personal data on the terms below.

## What data

- name;
- email address;
- Telegram username, if I provide it;
- the topic and text of my message;
- the website language;
- technical data for spam protection: a one-way hash of my IP address and the time taken to fill in the form.

## Purpose

To answer my request, contact me about it and protect the form from spam.

## What the operator may do with the data

Collect, record, organize, store, update, retrieve, use, transfer to the service providers listed below, restrict,
delete and destroy it — with or without automated means.

## Who processes the data on the operator's behalf

- Supabase, Inc. — stores messages (servers in the EU, Germany);
- Telegram — delivers a notification with the message to the operator's private chat;
- the operator's email service provider — to send the reply.

My data may be processed outside my country of residence, as described in the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/) (section “International transfers”).

## How long

Until the purpose is achieved and for **one year** after the operator's last reply, or until I withdraw my consent,
whichever comes first.

## Withdrawing consent

I can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to support@karmanto.com. My data will then be deleted within
30 days. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

Giving consent is voluntary. Instead of using the form, I can write directly to support@karmanto.com.

Everything not covered here is described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/).
