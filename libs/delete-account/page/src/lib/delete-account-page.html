Delete Your Account

How to delete your Karmanto account and all your data in it.

In the app

  1. Open Karmanto and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

On iPhone and iPad, if you signed in with Apple, the app will then ask you to confirm with Apple: this disconnects the app from your Apple ID.

What is deleted

Your account and all your words, lists and progress are deleted on all your devices, immediately and permanently. This can't be undone.
